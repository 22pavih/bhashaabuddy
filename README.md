# Bhasha Buddy 🗣️

**Breaking language barriers when moving to a new city.**

Bhasha Buddy is a multilingual AI assistant concept designed to help people navigate local services in unfamiliar cities, even when they don't speak the local language.

Finding a flat, contacting a landlord, or arranging everyday services can be challenging when communication becomes a barrier. Bhasha Buddy aims to make these interactions simpler through a conversational interface that helps users express their needs and communicate across languages.

**[🚀 Try the Live App](https://bhashaabuddy.lovable.app)**

## The Problem

Moving to a new city often means navigating unfamiliar languages while trying to secure housing and access essential services. Even routine tasks, such as asking about rent, deposits, availability, or household services, can become difficult when users and service providers speak different languages.

## The Solution

Bhasha Buddy explores how conversational AI and multilingual voice interactions could help bridge this gap.

### Example Use Case

Imagine moving to Bengaluru and looking for a flat in HSR Layout.

- **Your requirements:** A flat within a ₹25,000 budget.
- **Your challenge:** You need to communicate with a local broker who speaks Kannada.
- **The Bhasha Buddy concept:** Help communicate your requirements, obtain relevant property details, and make the process easier to navigate.

The same idea could extend to finding cooks, maids, plumbers, and other local services.

## Key Product Goals

- **Language accessibility:** Make local services easier to navigate across language barriers.
- **Conversational experience:** Let users express their needs naturally.
- **Practical utility:** Focus on everyday challenges associated with settling into a new city.
- **Scalable use cases:** Explore applications beyond housing, including other essential local services.
